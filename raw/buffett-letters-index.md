## How to use for wiki ingest

Letters are long (~20–50 pages each). Strategy for `value-investing.md`, `behavioral-finance.md`, and `traders-and-thinkers.md`:

1. **Priority reads** for methodology extraction:
   - 1977 (first letter — framework-setting)
   - 1983 (intrinsic value)
   - 1987 (Mr. Market allegory, value vs. growth)
   - 1996 (owner's manual section)
   - 2001 (post-bubble reflections)
   - 2008 (financial crisis, greed/fear)
   - 2013 (see's Candies, durability of competitive advantage)
   - 2023 / 2024 (most recent views on capital allocation and succession)

2. For each letter we ingest, save a separate file in `raw/` named `buffett-letter-YYYY.md` with extracted quotes relevant to methodology.

3. Manual step: these are PDFs from 2004 onward. WebFetch cannot retrieve PDFs. **User or Claude (via a future tool) must download these and save as `raw/buffett-letter-YYYY.pdf`** or convert to text and save as `.md`.
