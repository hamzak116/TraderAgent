## How the Economic Machine Works framework (summary from prior search)

Three forces drive the economy:

1. **Productivity growth** — long-run uptrend from innovation, efficiency, learning
2. **Short-term debt cycle** (~5–10 years) — credit expansion → spending → inflation → tightening → contraction → easing. The business cycle.
3. **Long-term debt cycle** (~50–75 years) — debt accumulates relative to income; eventually must deleverage via one of four paths (austerity, debt defaults/restructurings, central bank printing money, wealth transfers)

Credit is central — it allows spending pulled forward from future. Cycles emerge because debt must eventually be repaid, causing future spending to fall.
