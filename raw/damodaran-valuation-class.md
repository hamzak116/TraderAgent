## Overview

25-session webcast series (12–20 minutes each) supplementing Damodaran's valuation textbook. Each session has slides, a post-class test with solutions, and referenced textbook chapters (*Investments Valuation, 3rd Edition*).

## Core class sessions

| # | Title | Key topics |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Valuation | Rationale for valuation; bias, complexity, uncertainty challenges |
| 2 | Intrinsic Value — Foundations | Business versus equity valuation distinction |
| 3 | The Risk Free Rate | Requirements and estimation across currencies |
| 4 | The Equity Risk Premium | Mature/emerging market approaches; individual company application |
| 5 | Betas — Relative Risk Measures | Beta measurement; regression critique; firm-level estimation |
| 6 | Costs of Debt & Capital | Debt definition; cost of capital computation |
| 7 | Estimating Cash Flows | Earnings → reinvestment → cash flow conversion |
| 8 | Estimating Growth | Historical, analyst, and fundamental growth approaches |
| 9 | Terminal Value | "Biggest number" in DCF; constraint rules |
| 10 | Value Enhancement | Drivers and management impact on firm value |
| 11 | Loose Ends in Valuation | Cash, cross-holdings, debt adjustments to equity value |
| 12 | Acquisition Ornaments | Control, synergy, complexity effects |
| 13 | Loose Ends II: Distress, Dilution & Illiquidity | Per-share value adjustments |
| 14 | Relative Valuation — First Principles | Four-step multiples deconstruction |
| 15 | PE Ratios | Determinants; cross-time and cross-company comparisons |
| 16 | Other Earnings Multiples | Operating earnings and EBITDA multiples |
| 17 | Book Value Multiples | P/B and EV-to-invested-capital variations |
| 18 | Revenue Multiples | Business-specific revenue multiple determinants |
| 19 | Asset Based Valuation | Accounting book value; sum-of-parts approaches |
| 20 | Private Company Valuation | Small and large privately-held business challenges |
| 21 | The Essence of Real Options | Asset-as-options framework |
| 22 | The Option to Delay | Patents and undeveloped reserves valuation |
| 23 | The Option to Expand & Abandon | Growth and flexibility value in young companies |
| 24 | Distressed Equity as an Option | Troubled firms with debt behaving as call options |
| 25 | Closing Thoughts | Course wrap-up and synthesis |
