## SEPA Framework Overview

SEPA (Specific Entry Point Analysis) integrates four components that must align simultaneously:

- **S — Specific Entry Point**: Precise entry timing via VCP patterns and pivot point breakouts
- **E — Earnings**: Fundamental screening for accelerating growth
- **P — Price Action**: Stage 2 uptrend confirmation using Trend Template
- **A — Announcement/Catalyst**: Fundamental drivers sustaining price advances

**Minervini quote**: *"When dealing with growth stocks, really only three things matter: earnings, sales, and margins."*

## Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP)

### Pattern Structure
- Multiple consolidations (typically 3–4) with progressively smaller pullbacks
- Example: contractions measuring 18% → 12% → 6% demonstrate proper tightening
- Final contraction displays 3–5% volatility before breakout

### Entry Requirements
- Entry at the pivot point (resistance from most recent contraction)
- Breakout volume must be 40–50% above average daily volume
- Follow-through volume: 30–40% above average for 2–5 days

### Volume Characteristics During Formation
- Declining volume during each pullback (supply exhausting)
- Expanding volume on rallies (demand building)
- Surge on breakout day (institutional confirmation)

## The Eight-Point Trend Template

All criteria must be met for Stage 2 confirmation:

1. Price above 50-day moving average
2. Price above 150-day moving average
3. Price above 200-day moving average
4. 50-day MA positioned above 150-day MA
5. 150-day MA positioned above 200-day MA
6. 200-day MA trending upward (minimum 1 month, preferably 4–5 months)
7. Price within 25% of 52-week high
8. Relative Strength rating above 70 (ideally 90+)

**Impact**: These criteria eliminate approximately 95% of stocks from consideration.

## Four Stages of Price Movement

- **Stage 1 — Accumulation**: Sideways price action; 200-day MA flattening; institutional quiet buying begins
- **Stage 2 — Advancement**: Clear uptrend with higher highs/lows; all MA alignment proper; VCP patterns form here; *SEPA operates exclusively during this stage*
- **Stage 3 — Distribution**: Price stalls at highs; increased volatility; 200-day MA flattens; institutions distribute to late retail buyers
- **Stage 4 — Decline**: Lower lows/highs; price below 200-day MA; avoid entirely; no SEPA setups occur here

## Earnings Analysis Criteria

### Fundamental Screening Thresholds
- **Quarterly EPS Growth**: Minimum 20% year-over-year; higher percentages (40–50%+) preferred
- **Revenue Growth**: Minimum 15% year-over-year; accelerating trends confirm strength
- **Profit Margins**: Must expand quarter-over-quarter; stable or contracting margins raise sustainability concerns

### Institutional Ownership
- Optimal range: 30–70% of shares outstanding
- Below 30%: insufficient support for sustained advances
- Above 80–90%: saturated; few additional buyers remain

### Catalyst Types
- FDA approvals or regulatory clearances
- Major contract wins with multi-year revenue
- New product launches entering large markets
- Market share gains versus competitors
- Strategic partnerships with established leaders
- Industry tailwinds benefiting entire sectors

## Position Sizing & Risk Management

### Stop-Loss Rule
- **Standard stop**: 7–8% below entry price
- Non-negotiable rule; widening stops converts small losses to large losses

### Position Sizing Formula

```
Position Size = (Account Size × Risk %) ÷ (Entry Price − Stop Price)
```

**Example**: $100,000 account risking 1% on $50 entry with $46 stop:
- Position Size = $1,000 ÷ $4 = 250 shares

### Risk Per Trade
- Never risk more than 1–2% of total capital per single position
- Mathematical consistency ensures asymmetric returns across multiple trades

**Minervini quote**: *"I went from having a 15% loss normalised everything to a 10% stop and my account would have been up 72% instead of being down."*

## Market Environment Integration

### Optimal Conditions
- ~90% of successful breakouts occur when major indices trade above monthly 10-period exponential moving averages
- Bullish conditions: indices above 50-day and 200-day MAs with expanding breadth

### Neutral Conditions
- Reduce position sizing; focus only on highest-quality setups
- Take partial profits faster; trail stops more aggressively

### Bearish Conditions
- Move to cash entirely
- **Minervini quote**: *"When the market's in a correction, I go to cash. I don't fight it."*

## Systematic Application Workflow

1. **Screen**: Apply Trend Template to identify Stage 2 candidates
2. **Verify**: Confirm 20%+ earnings growth with accelerating trends
3. **Identify**: Research catalysts and fundamental drivers
4. **Monitor**: Track qualified stocks for VCP pattern formation
5. **Enter**: Execute on pivot breakout with 40–50% above-average volume
6. **Manage**: Monitor daily; execute stop at −7/−8%; trail with moving averages

## Common Implementation Errors

- Entering before volume-confirmed breakout
- Ignoring fundamental requirements despite perfect charts
- Trading during bearish market environments
- Moving stop-losses away from planned levels
- Insufficient position sizing relative to account risk tolerance

## Key Distinguishing Principles

This methodology counters conventional wisdom by:
- Buying stocks making new highs (not seeking bargains)
- Cutting losses quickly at 7–8% (not hoping and holding)
- Concentrating in few high-conviction positions (not broad diversification)
