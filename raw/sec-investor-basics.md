## Investment product categories (11 covered on site)

- **Foundational securities**: Stocks, bonds
- **Pooled vehicles**: Mutual funds, ETFs, hedge funds
- **Fixed-income**: Municipal bonds, CDs, annuities
- **Cash-equivalent**: Money market funds
- **Real assets**: Commodities, REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)
- **Geographic**: International investing options

## Core risk concepts

"The value of stocks, bonds, and other securities fluctuates with market conditions." No investment guarantees returns; securities may lose value.

## Diversification strategy

Asset allocation and diversification are emphasized as core getting-started topics. Purpose: spread risk across multiple investment types.

## Fraud prevention resources

The SEC dedicates substantial resources to investor protection:

- Types of fraud identification
- Avoidance strategies and warning signs
- Resources for fraud victims
- Retirement-specific fraud warnings for older investors

## Essential educational topics (SEC's five-area roadmap)

1. Saving and investing fundamentals
2. How stock markets operate
3. Understanding investment fees
4. Working with investment professionals
5. Conducting proper investment research

## Core message

"Financial security" requires **long-term planning and informed decision-making rather than speculation.**
